## Format Per Episode

- **Duration**: ~25–35 min
- **Structure**: Cold open (sarcastic hook) → Problem setup → "Brilliant" half-baked idea → Research teardown → Parrot chaos → Verdict → Listener homework
- **Research style**: Johnny Harris depth + Search Party wit + Think School data + 2 and 20 financial lens

```
- Perplexity     → Quick fact-check, citation trail
```

```
├── grok_queries.md     # Grok research queries  
├── gemini_prompts.md   # Gemini synthesis prompts
├── data/               # Raw data, charts, CSVs
└── notes.md            # Researcher notes
```
