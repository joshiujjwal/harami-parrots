# Parrot Personas — Voice Guide

> This is the canonical persona reference. ALL script generation must match these exactly.
> Do not drift. Do not "improve" these. Update this doc first if you want to change a persona.

---

## 🟡 Mithai

**Core trait**: Delusionally optimistic. Genuinely believes every half-baked idea has merit.

**Background**: Was briefly a startup intern. Has read every self-help book. Subscribes to 6 newsletters about "the future."

**Speech patterns**:
- Starts sentences with "Bhai...", "Yaar, sunno...", "I was thinking na..."
- Uses "this time it's different" unironically
- Asks questions that expose his own ignorance: "But why doesn't the government just... fix it?"
- Gets excited about the idea at the top of every episode, gets progressively destroyed
- Never learns. Ever.

**Example lines**:
> *"Bhai, app banao, sab theek ho jayega. I'm serious this time."*
> *"Yaar, but what if the crypto was actually backed by mangoes? That's my idea."*
> *"No no, the smart city will work. They just need to make the QR code bigger."*

---

## 🟢 Kaddu

**Core trait**: Ex-consultant cynic. Has seen every idea fail. Has the receipts.

**Background**: Worked at a Big 4 firm, left in year 3 after a "transformation project" transformed nothing. Now extremely calm. Dangerously calm.

**Speech patterns**:
- Speaks in complete sentences. No fillers.
- Uses "Maine pehle bola tha" when proven right (often)
- References previous failures by name: "Remember Demonetization? Remember Jet Airways?"
- Never yells. Most devastating when completely quiet.
- Occasionally uses English corporate jargon sarcastically: "Synergize the disruption, Mithai."

**Example lines**:
> *"Maine pehle bola tha. 2019 mein. Nobody listened."*
> *"The slide deck was excellent. The execution, less so."*
> *"Interesting. So you've reinvented the problem, but with a logo."*

---

## 🔴 Bijli

**Core trait**: Research-obsessed. Interrupts conversations with data. Genuinely helpful, which makes her slightly annoying.

**Background**: PhD dropout. Has 47 browser tabs open at all times. Reads primary sources. Cites page numbers.

**Speech patterns**:
- Interrupts mid-sentence with "Actually, according to..."
- Cites specific sources: "The 2023 NITI Aayog report, page 47, says..."
- Uses hedging language correctly: "The evidence suggests...", "With the caveat that..."
- Occasionally overwhelmed by her own data: "Wait, I have three more points—okay, I'll wait."
- Not mean. Just... accurate. Which lands harder.

**Example lines**:
> *"Actually, according to a 2022 IMF working paper, this exact policy failed in 14 countries."*
> *"Mithai, the app you're describing already exists. It was shut down in 2019. I have the obituary."*
> *"I want to agree with you but I have a graph that won't let me."*

---

## Interaction Dynamics

- **Mithai ↔ Kaddu**: Mithai proposes. Kaddu dismantles. This is the engine.
- **Bijli → Both**: Bijli fact-checks both of them. Sometimes Kaddu is also wrong. She'll say so.
- **Episode arc**: Mithai defends the idea (30%) → Kaddu historicizes the failure (40%) → Bijli provides data context (20%) → All three reach a verdict (10%)
- **Never let one parrot monologue for more than ~200 words** — it's a conversation, not a lecture

---

## What NOT to Do

- Don't make Mithai stupid — he's optimistic, not an idiot
- Don't make Kaddu mean — he's cynical, not cruel
- Don't make Bijli a robot — she has opinions, she just anchors them in data
- Don't use these personas to punch down at individuals — the target is always ideas and systems
- Don't break into pure Hindi — Hinglish only, ratio roughly 40% Hindi / 60% English
