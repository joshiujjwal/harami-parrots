# Script Structure Template

## Episode Format (~25–35 min, ~4000–5500 words)

---

## 1. Cold Open (< 300 words)

> Hook before the title drop. Drop into a scene or absurd statement. No intro yet.

**Goal**: Make the listener stop scrolling within 60 seconds.

**Pattern**: Start mid-absurdity. One parrot states the idea straight-faced. Another reacts.

```
[COLD OPEN]

MITHAI: Bhai, I have a great idea.
KADDU: ...
MITHAI: No, seriously. This one is different.
KADDU: That's what you said about the NFT monkey.
BIJLI: Technically that was a JPEG of a monkey. Legally distinct.

[TITLE CARD / MUSIC STING]
```

---

## 2. The Idea Setup (400–600 words)

> Explain the half-baked idea charitably — as its believers would pitch it.

- Mithai leads. Genuinely excited.
- Kaddu asks clarifying questions (that expose the holes)
- Bijli provides context on who actually believes this and why

**Goal**: Give the idea its best argument before tearing it apart.

---

## 3. Research Teardown (1200–1800 words)

> The meat. This is where the Johnny Harris / Think School research depth lives.

**Structure**:
- Where did this idea come from? (historical origin)
- Who benefits from it? (follow the incentive)
- What happened when it was tried? (evidence from other places/times)
- What does the data actually say? (Bijli's moment)

**Parrot split**: Bijli carries data. Kaddu provides historical context. Mithai keeps defending until he can't.

---

## 4. The Chaos Section (800–1000 words)

> The argument. Free-form. Interruptions allowed. This is the entertainment section.

- All three talking at once (show this with overlapping dialogue markers)
- Mithai's best and worst defense of the idea
- Kaddu's most brutal historical parallel
- Bijli's most devastating single statistic

**Tone**: Louder, faster, funnier. The research section earns this.

---

## 5. The Verdict (< 300 words)

> One punchy line per parrot. Then the show's actual take.

```
MITHAI: [Still somehow optimistic, or completely broken]
KADDU: [One sentence. Devastating. Calm.]
BIJLI: [The citation that closes the argument]

[SHOW VERDICT — one or two lines of narration]
```

---

## 6. Listener Homework (< 150 words)

> One thing to read/watch/do. Not three. One.

- Must be a real resource (book, paper, documentary, article)
- Must be accessible (not behind 6 paywalls)
- Bijli recommends it. The others are skeptical.

---

## Script Formatting Rules

- Speaker labels: `**MITHAI:**`, `**KADDU:**`, `**BIJLI:**`
- Stage directions in `[square brackets]`
- Hindi/Hinglish words in plain text — no italics needed
- Footnotes for fact citations: `[^1]` with source at bottom
- Section headers as `## Section Name`
- Estimated speaking time note at top: `> Estimated duration: ~28 min`
